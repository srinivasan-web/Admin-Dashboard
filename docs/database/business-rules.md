# Business rules and workflows

| Rule/workflow | Classification |
| --- | --- |
| A Customer is a commerce/CRM identity, separate from an administrative User. The PDF shows name, phone, and email in order and warranty workflows, but does not prove a User relationship or specify a contact-required rule. Email and phone, when supplied, are individually unique. Customers archive through soft deletion; no status vocabulary, address ownership, Order, Lead, or Warranty relationship is implemented in this scope. | **[PDF]** + **[TECHNICAL DECISION]** + **[CONFIRMATION REQUIRED]** |
| Product Type has name, slug, Active status, and description; its lifecycle is Active/Inactive, represented by `is_active` with an active default. The PDF shows no sort ordering or delete-retention rule. | **[PDF]** + **[TECHNICAL DECISION]** |
| Category has name, slug, image, status, and description. The PDF does not show a parent selector or hierarchy, so it is a flat master. Its Active/Inactive lifecycle is represented by `is_active`, active by default; no sort order or retention rule is shown. | **[PDF]** + **[TECHNICAL DECISION]** |
| HSN title is visibly required and type is optional. The PDF supplies SGST and IGST multiplier controls but no calculation, range, default, lifecycle, or deletion semantics; these values are stored as nullable two-decimal values without behavior. When Products are approved, their `hsn_code_id` should use a restrictive FK delete policy so a referenced classification cannot be removed. | **[PDF]** + **[TECHNICAL DECISION]** |
| Products can be searched by name/SKU/ID, filtered by type, exported, edited, duplicated, deleted, and tagged. SKU is globally unique; Product has no slug because none is shown. Brand, category, and product type are mandatory catalogue classifications; HSN is optional. MRP and optional sale price are stored as two-decimal money values with no price or tax calculation rule. Product records soft delete for future historical safety; referenced masters are restricted from deletion. | **[PDF]** + **[TECHNICAL DECISION]** |
| Product images are ordered per Product and may designate one primary image. The PDF does not define an image-type vocabulary, so `type` is a required free-text value rather than an enum. Product deletion cascades attachment rows only on a physical delete; Media deletion is restricted while attached. | **[PDF]** + **[TECHNICAL DECISION]** |
| A Product Variant belongs to exactly one Product and carries only the shown label, value, and SKU. Variant SKU is unique among variants and a label/value pair is unique within its Product. Variants soft delete for future history; physical Product deletion cascades them. Variant pricing, status, and stock behavior remain unsupported. | **[PDF]** + **[TECHNICAL DECISION]** |
| Warehouse Stock is grouped by product with variants shown per row, filtered by All/Por/Trichy, and has update/sync actions. `warehouse_stocks` holds one current Product-level row per Warehouse/Product pair; zero is valid and negative quantity is prevented. Parent deletion is restricted while stock exists. Reservations, reorder policy, variant ownership, movement history, and sync behavior remain undefined. Future stock mutation must run in a transaction and lock the current row (`lockForUpdate`) before changing quantity. | **[PDF]** + **[TECHNICAL DECISION]** + **[CONFIRMATION REQUIRED]** |
| `warehouse_stocks.quantity` is the current balance; `stock_movements` is append-only history. Each event has a non-zero signed delta and a free-text movement type because no approved type vocabulary exists. It references the WarehouseStock row rather than duplicating Product/Warehouse data; reference polymorphism is deferred until a source domain exists. Eloquent updates/deletes are blocked, parent stock deletion is restricted, and deleting an actor nulls `created_by` without removing history. | **[TECHNICAL DECISION]** + **[CONFIRMATION REQUIRED]** |
| A buy-it-with combo targets either one product or a product type; the UI recommends at most four add-on products. | **[PDF]** |
| Combo is the Buy-It-With configuration header: its required name stores the visible custom title, and its Active/Inactive lifecycle uses `is_active` with an active default. `combo_items` holds its add-on Products with positive quantity. A Combo/Product pair is unique; deleting a Combo cascades items, while a referenced Product is restricted from physical deletion. Targeting and max-four enforcement remain deferred. | **[PDF]** + **[TECHNICAL DECISION]** |
| Featured placements are maintained independently for “Choose Your Product” and “Explore Bestsellers,” grouped by product type. | **[PDF]** |
| Coupon supports fixed and percentage discounts; availability depends on status, validity period, amount thresholds, usage limit, product-type target, website visibility and optional UTM source. Exact calculation/usage semantics are **[CONFIRMATION REQUIRED]**. | **[PDF]** + **[CONFIRMATION REQUIRED]** |
| Only testimonials that are featured, active, and have an image appear on the landing carousel; at most six are shown, ordered by highest rating. | **[PDF]** |
| Testimonial CSV requires selected product/source; visible columns are id, profileName, text, date, title, rating, images. Images are comma-separated URLs; rating accepts 1–5 or “x out of 5”; date is yyyy-mm-dd. | **[PDF]** |
| FAQ may be general (no product type), has required question/answer, sort order and active status. | **[PDF]** |
| Legal pages are edited per currently selected brand; listed pages include Terms, Privacy, Limited Warranty and HDFC Terms. | **[PDF]** |
| Brand is selectable on the product form and scopes legal-page editing. Brand lifecycle, media/logo management, status, ordering, and descriptions are not specified. | **[PDF]** + **[CONFIRMATION REQUIRED]** |
| Banners target Landing or Listing page, require title and desktop banner, and support mobile banner, ordering, redirect and schedule. | **[PDF]** |
| Order screens distinguish regular and “Abandon” views and expose edit/hold, payment verification/details, logistics, comments, email resend and KinetIQ push. State transitions/integration behavior are **[CONFIRMATION REQUIRED]**. | **[PDF]** + **[CONFIRMATION REQUIRED]** |
| An Order is a historical transaction header. It belongs to one Customer and may reference one Warehouse. Each Order has Product item snapshots and at most one immutable billing and shipping snapshot, enforced by `UNIQUE(order_id, address_type)`. A later physical Product deletion nulls `product_id` without losing the SKU/name/price snapshot; physical Customer, Warehouse, or Order deletion is restricted while referenced. | **[PDF]** + **[TECHNICAL DECISION]** |
| Approved lifecycle: Order starts `pending`, then may move through `confirmed`, `processing`, and `completed`; active states may cancel, `pending` may become `abandoned`, and terminal states are completed/cancelled/abandoned. Payment starts `pending`; it may authorize, pay, fail, or refund where applicable. Fulfillment starts `pending`; it may process, ship, deliver, or cancel where applicable. Completion requires paid payment unless a later explicitly approved non-payment flow applies; delivery requires a valid completion path. Hold/resume preserves the prior valid active state. | **[CONFIRMED BUSINESS DECISION]** |
| `CreateOrder` only creates the approved initial `pending` values for Order, payment, and fulfillment. It persists the header, Product name/SKU/unit-price snapshots, billing/shipping snapshots, computed subtotal/discount/total, and first Order status-history row inside one database transaction. It does not transition statuses, call a payment provider, mutate inventory, or perform fulfillment. | **[TECHNICAL DECISION]** + **[CONFIRMED BUSINESS DECISION]** |
| Order, payment, and fulfillment statuses are stored as free text to retain the approved vocabulary without database enums. Status changes must append an immutable history record with optional User actor. Item/address amendment authority, stock deduction, payment capture, and fulfillment operations remain outside this implementation. | **[CONFIRMED BUSINESS DECISION]** + **[TECHNICAL DECISION]** |
| CRM stages displayed: New Lead, Initial Contact, Interested, Hot Leads, Customer, Not Answered, Drop Off. Dashboard additionally reports due-today and overdue follow-ups. Allowed transitions are **[CONFIRMATION REQUIRED]**. | **[PDF]** + **[CONFIRMATION REQUIRED]** |
| Warranty records have a status and admin notes, but allowed statuses/transition authority are **[CONFIRMATION REQUIRED]**. | **[PDF]** + **[CONFIRMATION REQUIRED]** |
